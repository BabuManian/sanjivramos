🏗 Build & place

What you build here goes on your 🔨 Build hotbar slot. Hold it, aim where you want it (green = OK) and click. R turns it, B picks the next thing, and with 🔨 in your hand E picks a placed thing back up.

🎒 Sack

Unload logs, stone, food and batteries at the camp pile. Junk (bolts, pipes, tires, radios) goes in the grinder on the workbench.

🧰 Chest
