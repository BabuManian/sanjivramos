How to play

Hits raise a fighter's damage %. The higher it is, the further they fly. Knock rivals past the edge of the screen to take a stock. Last one standing wins (or most KOs when time runs out).

Keys 1A/D move · W or Space jump · S down/fast-fall · F attack · G special · H or Left Shift shield
Keys 2Arrows move (↑ jumps) · K or . attack · L or / special · ; or Right Shift shield (numpad 1/2/3 also work)
GamepadStick move (up = jump) · A attack · B special · X/Y jump · bumpers/triggers shield · right stick = smash attacks · Start pause
TouchLeft/right pads: stick + ATK, SPC, JMP, SHD buttons. Two touch players can share one tablet.
Smash attackTap a direction and attack at the same time (or press attack + special together, or right stick). Hold attack to charge.
SpecialsSpecial alone, or with ← →, ↑ (recovery) or ↓. Each fighter has 4.
DefenceShield blocks hits but shrinks; it breaks if it runs out. Shield + ←/→ rolls, shield + ↓ dodges, shield in the air air-dodges. Shield + attack grabs; then press a direction to throw.
EdgesFalling near a ledge grabs it. Up/jump climbs, attack climbs with a hit, down lets go. Down on a thin platform drops through.
Items❤️ heals · 💪 double damage · 👟 speed · 🍄 giant · ⭐ invincible · 💣 bomb, 🔫 blaster, 🏏 bat: press attack on them to pick up, attack to use, special to throw. Break the glowing Nova Core to charge your super, then press special!
