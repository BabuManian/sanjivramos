⬢ Hex Conquest

Grow your empire across a hex map. Place reinforcements, attack neighbours and take every capital.

1. Reinforce: tap your tiles to place new troops. 2. Attack: tap one of your tiles, then a neighbour. 3. Move: shift troops once between your tiles. Cities (🏰) give extra troops and defend better.
