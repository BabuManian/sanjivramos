SIGNAL LOST

Kestrel Base, Antarctica. Day 4 of the storm. The relief crew lost contact with the station after the drill team brought up something from 3 km under the ice.
You’re the relief crew. Get the generators running, call for evac, and get out.

Player 1 — WASD move · mouse look (click) · Shift run · E use (hold) · F flashlight · G throw flare · C crouch
Player 2 — ↑↓ move · ←→ turn · , . strafe · Enter use · / flashlight · ' flare · Right Shift run
Gamepads work for either player (A use · X light · B flare · LB run). Hold the flashlight on a crawler to stun it. Crawlers hate flares.
