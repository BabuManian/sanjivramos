🐍 Snake Brawl

Eat glowing food to grow. Make other snakes crash into your body — but don't hit theirs. When a snake crashes it turns into food. Biggest snake when time runs out wins!

Arrows / WASD to turn, hold Space to boost (costs length). Touch: swipe to turn, two fingers to boost. Online: up to 8 players, empty seats are CPU snakes.
