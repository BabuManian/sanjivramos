⚽ NEON SOCCER

Fast arcade football. Dribble, pass, shoot and outplay the CPU — or your friends on the same network.

Move: WASD / arrows / stick · Kick: Space (hold for power) · Sprint: Shift · Pass to a teammate by kicking toward them. Player 2 (one keyboard): arrows + Enter / right Shift. Your player shows your picture — online, everyone sees each other’s.
